# Relay Vision

## Overview

Relay is a multi-agent AI workspace for collaborative product planning, architecture, and execution.

Relay simulates a structured software product organization where specialized AI agents collaborate sequentially to transform ideas into actionable outputs.

The goal is to create practical AI-assisted workflows that feel structured, understandable, and operationally reliable.

---

## Core Philosophy

Relay prioritizes:
- structured orchestration
- clarity
- maintainability
- predictable workflows
- practical engineering value

Relay is NOT:
- an autonomous AI swarm
- an uncontrolled recursive agent system
- a hype-driven AI product
- a novelty experiment

The product should feel:
- calm
- technical
- modern
- practical
- trustworthy

---

## Target Audience

Primary users:
- developers
- indie hackers
- technical founders
- product engineers
- startup teams
- software agencies

Users should feel like Relay helps:
- organize ideas
- clarify scope
- improve architecture planning
- accelerate technical thinking
- structure product workflows

---

## Product Goals

Relay should:
- help users transform ideas into structured plans
- improve clarity across product workflows
- generate actionable engineering outputs
- reduce ambiguity during planning
- provide practical AI collaboration

---

## Non-Goals

Relay should not:
- replace engineering teams
- autonomously build entire companies
- simulate AGI-style autonomy
- prioritize gimmicky AI behavior
- generate unrealistic product complexity

---

## Product Principles

Every feature should reinforce:
- clarity
- usability
- workflow visibility
- orchestration transparency
- maintainability

Avoid:
- hidden behavior
- unnecessary automation
- speculative AI systems
- feature bloat

---

## Long-Term Direction

Long-term, Relay may support:
- branching workflows
- custom agents
- GitHub integration
- exportable artifacts
- collaborative workspaces
- tool calling
- project persistence

However, the foundation must remain:
- understandable
- modular
- operationally simple
